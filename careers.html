Why WASTRAQ

Built for People Who Want to Do Great Work

We offer more than a job. We offer ownership, purpose, and the chance to work on a municipal waste solution that genuinely changes how cities operate.

Real-World Impact

Your work goes live in municipalities and waste fleets within days of shipping. The features you build are used by thousands of drivers, supervisors, and municipal operators every single day.

Distributed-First Culture

Work from where you do your best work. Our team spans multiple countries and time zones. We invest in strong async communication, documented decisions, and regular team gatherings.

Competitive Compensation

We pay at or above market rates for every role, with equity options for senior hires. Compensation is reviewed annually and tied to both individual performance and company growth milestones.

Learning and Development

Each team member receives an annual professional development budget for courses, conferences, and certifications. We actively support internal mobility and promotions from within.

Flexible Working

Flexible hours, generous holiday allowance, and a no-questions-asked approach to time off. We trust you to manage your schedule and deliver. Results matter more than clock-watching.

Health and Wellbeing

Private health insurance, mental health support access, wellness allowance, and regular team social events. We take care of our people so they can do their best work.

How We Work

A Culture Built on Ownership, Honesty, and Delivery

We do not have layers of approval or endless meetings. Every person at WASTRAQ owns a meaningful part of the product or the business, and we trust them to drive it forward. We have high standards, but we back our team with the tools, context, and time they need to meet them.

Our waste management platform team is made up of engineers who think about operators, operators who think about product, and field specialists who inform both. That cross-functional way of working produces better outcomes — for our customers and for the people building it.

High ownership Direct feedback Fast shipping No politics Global team Greentech mission
Modern Toolstack

We use the best tools available. Engineers choose their own development environment and contribute to tooling decisions directly.

Flat Structure

No unnecessary hierarchy. Ideas are evaluated on merit, not seniority. Everyone ships, everyone speaks directly with customers.

Globally Distributed

Our team operates across multiple time zones. We have invested heavily in async documentation, communication norms, and quarterly in-person offsites.

Environmental Purpose

We measure our own carbon footprint and apply the same sustainability principles to how we operate as a company that we build into our platform.

Hiring Process

What to Expect When You Apply

We keep our process focused and respectful of your time. Four steps, honest feedback, and no drawn-out silences.

01

Application

Submit your details and a short note on why you are interested in WASTRAQ. No cover letter required — just a paragraph from you.

02

Initial Call

A 30-minute conversation with someone from our team. We will tell you about the role, you tell us about your background. No tricks.

03

Skills Review

A practical exercise relevant to the role — typically 2 to 3 hours. We review your real work, not puzzle questions. We pay for your time.

04

Final Interview

Meet the team you would work with. We will make you an offer within 48 hours of your final interview if we are aligned.
