— THE CHALLENGE

Waste Management in Kuppam
Before WASTRAQ

A municipality of 65,000 residents generating 35–40 metric tonnes of waste daily — with no digital visibility into any of it.

Untracked Waste

All collection data lived in paper registers. No digital record, no aggregation, no state-level reporting — errors were routine and audits were impossible.

Poor Monitoring

No GPS. Vehicles disappeared after departure. Supervisors had zero visibility into routes, missed stops, or vehicle location throughout the day.

Low Efficiency

Budgets were built on guesswork. SBM compliance reports took 3–5 days to compile manually. Data-driven decisions simply weren't possible.

— HOW WASTRAQ WORKS

From the Ground Up —
Every Step is Digital

A structured 12-step deployment delivered full digital coverage without disrupting Kuppam's daily waste operations.

01

Collect & Register

Field teams conducted a door-to-door baseline survey across all 30 wards — registering every household, commercial establishment, and bulk waste generator with a unique QR code profile in WASTRAQ.

  • 4,800+ residential households registered
  • 30+ bulk generators onboarded
  • 350+ commercial establishments mapped
  • All routes digitised with GIS boundary coordinates
02

Track in Real Time

Every collection vehicle was fitted with GPS trackers. Drivers scan QR codes at each household using the WASTRAQ mobile app — creating a verified digital record of every collection event, every trip, every day.

  • Live GPS for 100% of the fleet
  • QR scan proof at every household stop
  • Biometric + app-based worker attendance
  • Offline capability — works without internet
03

Monitor & Improve

The Municipal Commissioner monitors Kuppam's entire waste operation from a single live dashboard — collection heatmaps, worker attendance, MRF data, and SBM compliance reports available instantly, not days later.

  • Live collection status heatmaps by ward
  • Auto-generated SBM compliance reports
  • MRF inbound/outbound weight tracking via IoT
  • Citizen complaint portal with 48hr resolution
