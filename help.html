Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about the WASTRAQ smart waste management platform.

Most organisations are fully operational within 2 to 5 business days of signing up. Our dedicated onboarding team handles data migration, platform configuration, and hands-on training for your supervisors and drivers.

For enterprise and municipal deployments with complex route structures or ERP integrations, full onboarding typically takes 2 to 3 weeks. Your customer success manager will provide a detailed project timeline at kickoff.

WASTRAQ's RouteTraq AI engine analyses your collection zones, vehicle capacity, road network data, service frequencies, and collection point locations to generate mathematically optimized routes.

When IoT bin sensors are connected, routes are dynamically adjusted in real time based on actual fill levels — eliminating trips to empty bins and triggering urgent collections for overfull containers. Most customers achieve 25–35% fuel cost reductions within the first month.

Yes. WASTRAQ supports all major IoT bin sensor manufacturers via native connectors and our open REST API. Fill-level data, temperature readings, and tilt/tip events are ingested in real time and feed directly into the RouteTraq AI scheduling engine.

WASTRAQ offers native integrations with Xero, QuickBooks, SAP, and Oracle. Our full REST API also supports custom integrations with any ERP, CRM, or business intelligence platform your organisation already uses.

RouteTraq AI — Dynamic Fill-Level Re-Routing and Real-Time Schedule Adjustment

WASTRAQ v4.8.0 delivers a major leap in AI route optimization for waste collection fleets. Routes now update automatically in real time when connected bin sensors report fill levels above configured thresholds.

What Changed

Previously, RouteTraq AI generated optimized routes based on schedule frequency and zone data. With v4.8.0, the engine ingests live fill-level data from IoT sensors.

  • Bins above configured fill threshold are automatically added to active routes
  • Bins below minimum fill threshold are deprioritised and skipped
