Our Mission

Making Waste Management Smarter, Cleaner and More Profitable for Everyone

Every day, millions of waste collection vehicles drive routes that haven't been optimised in years. Bins get emptied before they're full. Missed collections trigger complaint calls. Billing errors create disputes. Drivers lack the tools to do their best work.

WASTRAQ exists to fix all of that — with intelligent software that transforms waste management from a reactive, manual operation into a proactive, data-driven business that's good for operators, good for cities, and good for the planet.

What We Stand For

Our Values Shape Everything We Build

These aren't words on a wall. They're the principles behind every product decision, every customer interaction, and every line of code we write.

Impact First

Every feature we build starts with one question: does this create real, measurable impact for our customers and the environment?

Radical Transparency

We believe in honesty — with our customers, our partners, and each other. No hidden fees, no vague promises, no marketing fluff.

People Over Processes

Technology should serve people, not the other way around. We design for the driver in the cab and the manager at the desk equally.

Relentless Improvement

Waste management is evolving fast. We iterate constantly, listen obsessively, and ship improvements every week — not every year.

Sustainability as Standard

Environmental responsibility isn't a feature we bolt on — it's built into the core of every route, every decision, every report.

Customer Obsession

Our success is measured by our customers' success. Their outcomes drive our roadmap, our support standards, and our culture.

Environmental Impact

Our Commitment to a Cleaner Planet

WASTRAQ isn't just a tool for managing waste — it's a force for reducing it. Every optimised route means fewer vehicle kilometres. Every sensor-driven collection means lower emissions. Every insight we surface helps operators make greener choices.
