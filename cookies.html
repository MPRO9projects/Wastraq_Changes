Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how WASTRAQ uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, analyze website traffic, and enhance our AI-powered smart waste management platform.

Effective Date: 26 June 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember user preferences, improve performance, and provide a better browsing experience.

2. How WASTRAQ Uses Cookies

WASTRAQ may use cookies to improve website functionality, remember user preferences, analyze visitor behavior, measure website performance, support security, and improve our platform experience.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand website traffic and user behavior.
  • Performance Cookies: Help improve website speed and usability.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember user choices such as consent preferences.
  • Marketing Cookies: May be used for campaign tracking and relevant communications.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may use trusted third-party tools such as analytics, advertising, or CRM platforms. These third parties may place cookies to help us measure website performance, improve campaigns, and understand user engagement.

5. Managing Cookie Preferences

You can accept or decline cookies through the cookie banner on our website. You can also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

6. Changes to This Cookie Policy

WASTRAQ may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
