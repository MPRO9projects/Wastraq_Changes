Partnership Tiers

Choose Your Partnership Model

Three distinct partnership pathways — each designed for a different type of business and growth ambition.

Reseller Partner

Sell WASTRAQ directly to your customers under your brand. Earn recurring commissions on every licence, with full sales and marketing support from our team.

  • Up to 30% recurring commission
  • Co-branded marketing materials
  • Dedicated partner success manager
  • Sales training and certification
  • Priority technical support

Referral Partner

Recommend WASTRAQ to businesses within your network. Earn a competitive referral fee for every successful introduction — with zero sales obligations.

  • Flat referral fee per qualified lead
  • Simple online referral portal
  • Real-time tracking of referred deals
  • No sales targets or quotas
  • Works for consultants, advisors, networks

Technology Partner

Integrate your product with WASTRAQ's open API to deliver combined value to shared customers. Co-develop, co-market, and grow together in the waste technology ecosystem.

  • Full API & developer sandbox access
  • Co-development opportunities
  • Joint go-to-market programmes
  • Marketplace listing on WASTRAQ Hub
  • Technical integration support
